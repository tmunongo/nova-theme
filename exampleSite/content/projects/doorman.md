Doorman is an open-source, privacy-first analytics micro-service engineered for zero-maintenance operation.

## Architecture Highlights

* Single binary deployment with embedded SQLite storage engine
* Under 15MB RAM baseline footprint
* Cookieless session hashing adhering strictly to privacy regulations
* In-memory buffer flush with deterministic persistence routines
