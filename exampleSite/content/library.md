A living shelf of books that shaped my worldview, engineering philosophy, and craft.

## Distributed Systems & Architecture

* **Designing Data-Intensive Applications** — *Martin Kleppmann*
  The gold standard for understanding storage engines, partition strategies, consensus protocols, and replication trade-offs.
* **Database Internals** — *Alex Petrov*
  A deep mechanical dive into B-Trees, LSM-trees, and storage layer design.
* **Building Microservices (2nd Edition)** — *Sam Newman*
  Pragmatic advice on service boundaries, event-driven decoupling, and evolutionary architecture.

## Software Engineering Craft & Philosophy

* **The Pragmatic Programmer** — *David Thomas & Andrew Hunt*
  Timeless heuristics on orthogonal design, tracer bullets, and developer discipline.
* **A Philosophy of Software Design** — *John Ousterhout*
  Deep vs shallow modules, cognitive load management, and defining complexity.
* **Site Reliability Engineering** — *Betsy Beyer, Chris Jones, Jennifer Petoff & Niall Richard Murphy*
  How Google runs production systems with error budgets and post-mortem culture.

## Science & Speculative Fiction

* **Blindsight** — *Peter Watts*
  A chilling exploration of consciousness vs intelligence in high-frontier space.
* **Stories of Your Life and Others** — *Ted Chiang*
  Masterful philosophical investigations disguised as short science fiction.
* **The Three-Body Problem** — *Cixin Liu*
  Cosmic sociological dread and the vast scale of physical physics.
